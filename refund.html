Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If something isn't right, here's how we handle returns and refunds.

Return Window

You have 30 days from the date of delivery to request a return. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and in the same condition you received them.

How to Request a Return

  • Contact us through our contact page with your order details
  • Include photos of the item if it arrived damaged
  • We'll provide return shipping instructions within 48 hours

Refund Processing

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we'll notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. Approved refunds will be processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Items marked as final sale
  • Personalized or custom-made products
  • Opened consumable goods

Exchanges

We only replace items if they are defective or damaged. If you need to exchange an item, please contact us and we'll guide you through the process.
